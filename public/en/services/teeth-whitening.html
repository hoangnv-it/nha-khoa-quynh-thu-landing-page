Common signs

When should you ask about whitening?

  • Yellowing from food, coffee or tea

  • Teeth darkening with age or from medication
  • Wanting a brighter smile before a big event

How does teeth whitening work?

Whitening removes dark stains that build up on teeth over time from food, medication and age. Teeth are whitened by a chemical reaction from the whitening gel, without thinning the enamel and without adding any coating to the tooth surface.

Whitening should be supervised by an experienced dentist to avoid burns to the gums and the lining of the mouth. At Quynh Thu Dental, the dentist examines you first and keeps an eye on you throughout the session.

FAQ

Questions about teeth whitening

How long does whitening take?

In-office blue light whitening takes about 60 minutes. If there's a lot of tartar or staining, the dentist may clean and polish your teeth first.

Does whitening thin my teeth?

No. Teeth are whitened by a chemical reaction from the gel, without thinning the enamel or adding any coating to the surface.

Will my crowns or fillings get whiter too?

No. Whitening only brightens natural teeth and doesn't change the color of crowns or fillings. The dentist will examine you and talk you through it before starting.
