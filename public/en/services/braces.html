Common signs

When should you come in?

  • Overbite, underbite or crowded teeth
  • Upper front teeth that bite behind the lower teeth
  • Your child is losing baby teeth and new teeth are coming in crooked, rotated or out of place
  • Gaps between the front teeth
  • A misaligned bite or uncomfortable chewing

These are common signs only. A dentist needs to examine you to diagnose the problem and recommend treatment.

The goal of braces

Orthodontic treatment should deliver both looks and function: straight teeth and a proper bite for comfortable chewing. Treatment usually takes about 2 years, depending on the case.

For crowded teeth, or overbites involving both the teeth and the jaw, the dentist may recommend removing premolars and placing mini-screws. Summer break is a convenient time for students to start braces.

FAQ

Questions about braces

When should kids get orthodontic treatment?

If your child's upper front teeth bite behind the lower ones, or new teeth come in crooked while baby teeth are falling out, bring them in early. Early treatment is usually simpler and less expensive, and some cases take just a few weeks to a few months.

How long do braces take?

It depends on your teeth. Braces usually take about 2 years. Early treatment for kids can take anywhere from a few weeks to about a year.

Can I pay for braces in installments?

Yes. You can pay for braces in monthly installments at 0% interest.
