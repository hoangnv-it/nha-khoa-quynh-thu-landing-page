Common signs

When should you ask about crowns?

  • A front tooth that has darkened from long-term pulp inflammation
  • A badly broken tooth, or a lot of tooth lost after decay or a root canal
  • An old crown with gaps, a dark gumline, food getting stuck or bleeding gums
  • Gaps between teeth that have been filled over and over
  • Missing teeth that need a bridge or a removable denture

These are common signs only. A dentist needs to examine you to diagnose the problem and recommend treatment.

Restorations we offer

Porcelain crowns (all-ceramic, zirconia): cover badly broken or root-canal-treated teeth, restoring chewing strength and contact with the neighboring teeth.

Porcelain bridges: replace missing teeth. A standard bridge takes about 2-3 days. The Oval Pontic technique reshapes the gumline so the new tooth looks like it grows right out of the gum and traps less food; it takes about 2 weeks.

Veneers, onlays and overlays: fix gaps or uneven teeth, or restore part of a tooth without grinding away healthy enamel.

Removable dentures: for older adults missing many teeth. The denture is shaped around the anatomy of your mouth so it stays put while you eat.

FAQ

Questions about crowns

How long do crowns take?

It depends on the case. A simple crown can be done in about 1-2 days, a standard bridge in about 2-3 days, and the Oval Pontic technique in about 2 weeks. If an infected root needs treatment first, it can take a few months.

Do crowns come with a warranty?

Yes. The porcelain comes with an origin card and a warranty card. Premium crowns carry a 10-year warranty, and some types 12 years.

What if my old crown has gaps or my gums bleed?

The dentist examines you, cleans your teeth, may remove the old crown and treats the gum inflammation first, then makes a new crown that fits snugly at the gumline.
