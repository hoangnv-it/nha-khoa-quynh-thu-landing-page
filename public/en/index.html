FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are your hours?

Both locations: Monday to Saturday 8 AM to 7 PM, Sunday 8 AM to 6 PM.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is a good idea so you don't have to wait. But it's not required: you're always welcome to walk in for a consultation.

When should my child have their first dental visit?
  • Ideal timing: within 6 months after the first tooth comes in, or by 12 months at the latest.
  • Don't skip: ages 6 - 12, when baby teeth make way for permanent teeth.

Bring your child in for a checkup every 6 months. Caring for teeth early is an investment in your child's healthy smile for years to come.

Do you offer 0% financing for braces?

Yes. We offer 0% interest installment plans for braces. Call or message us on Zalo for details.

Can you help with medical records and a VAT invoice for an insurance claim?

Yes. We can provide your medical records and issue an official VAT invoice for your insurance claim.
