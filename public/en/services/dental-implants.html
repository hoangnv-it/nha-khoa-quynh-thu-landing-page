Common signs

When should you ask about implants?

  • One or a few missing teeth you'd like to replace with fixed teeth
  • A full arch of missing teeth, and you want a fixed bridge for the whole arch
  • A tooth cracked down into the root that can't be saved
  • An infected, damaged root that no longer works for chewing

These are common signs only. A dentist needs to examine you to diagnose the problem and recommend treatment.

How do dental implants work?

An implant is placed in the jawbone to replace the missing root. After placement, it takes a few months for the implant to bond firmly with the bone before the final porcelain crown goes on.

For an infected root, the dentist may remove it, clean out the infection and wait 4-6 months for new bone to fill the socket before placing the implant: this costs less than extracting and placing an implant on the same day. Crowns on implants can use the Oval Pontic technique, which reshapes the gumline so the tooth looks like it grows right out of the gum.

FAQ

Questions about dental implants

How much does a dental implant cost?

Price guide: Korean implants 10,000,000 VND each, American implants 12,000,000 - 15,000,000 VND each, Swiss implants 20,000,000 VND each. All-ceramic crowns on implants are 5,000,000 - 8,000,000 VND per unit. The dentist examines you and gives you a quote before starting.

How long do dental implants take?

After the implant is placed, it takes a few months to bond firmly with the bone before the final porcelain crown goes on. The exact timeline depends on your case, and the dentist will let you know at your exam.

What is All-on-6?

All-on-6 restores a full arch of missing teeth by placing 6 implants in the jawbone to support a fixed porcelain bridge for the whole arch. It replaces all the missing teeth without an implant for every single tooth, while staying stable for chewing.

If a damaged tooth needs to come out, should I get an implant right away?

It depends. For an infected root, the dentist may remove it, clean out the infection and place the implant 4-6 months later, once new bone fills the socket. This costs less than extracting and placing an implant on the same day.
